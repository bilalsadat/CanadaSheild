PART 01

Executive summary

The opportunity. Canadians reported roughly $638–643M in fraud losses to the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre in 2024 — a near-300% rise since 2020 — and the CAFC estimates only 5–10% of fraud is ever reported, implying true losses in the multiple billions. Bill C-15 received Royal Assent on 26 March 2026: every Canadian bank must now detect and prevent consumer-targeted fraud, obtain express consent for risky capabilities, let customers set limits, and report fraud data to the FCAC. The regulatory clock has started and it is measured in quarters.

The insight. Every major competitor is an English-first product designed for a tech-comfortable American. The people actually losing the money are seniors, newcomers and linguistic communities (CRA, IRCC, job and investment scams in Punjabi, Mandarin, Cantonese, Tagalog, Arabic…), and small businesses (deepfake-executive wire fraud). The mismatch between who is targeted and who is served is the founding wedge.

The product. KinShield is not another single-channel detector. It is the orchestration layer above the platforms: one Trust Score (1–100) behind every channel — texts, links, calls, money, meetings — that knows the household, speaks its twelve languages, fuses every signal into one judged verdict, decides what to do, and stays with the family through the aftermath. Detection is becoming a commodity; judgment, family context, Canadian context, and recovery are not.

Already built & working
  • A real Trust Engine (5 signal families, transparent combiner, 12-language output) running on-device
  • A native iPhone app (Expo / React Native) — Dashboard, Protect, Map, Family, 30-feature catalogue
  • A web app / PWA sharing the same engine
  • Real camera QR scanning, clipboard checking, live community number lookup, calibration reporting
The ask
  • Stage 0 validate: $8–18K, 0–3 months
  • Stage 1 MVP: $90–160K bootstrap / $250–350K pre-seed, 3–9 months
  • Stage 2 seed & scale: $1.5–2.5M, 9–24 months
  • Stage 3 Series A & category: $8–15M, years 2–4
The one-sentence thesis. Build the P0 wedge for the undefended Canadian household, earn the evidence with published accuracy, and let a 30-feature catalogue compound behind six moats incumbents cannot cheaply copy — while a Bill-C-15 SDK turns the same brain into institutional revenue.
PART 02

The product & design — and what is already built

2.1 Design language

A restrained, professional dark system — deep ink background, layered surfaces, a single confident emerald accent, and red reserved strictly for threats. Vector iconography (no emoji-as-UI), a real typographic scale, the Trust Score dial as the product's signature object, and a Senior Mode presentation layer (larger type, simplified actions, spoken verdicts) that every feature must render into. Brand promise, printed on the product: "What's yours, we cannot read. What's the scammer's, we share to protect everyone."

PART 05

The features — how each works in the real app

★ = no consumer competitor offers it today. Phase: P0 = MVP (months 0–9) · P1 = scale (9–24) · P2 = years 2–3.

Detect & Decide

Trust Engine & Score ★ P0

Five signal families (content, artifact, authenticity, network, anomaly) fused by a transparent combiner into one 1–100 score + top-3 reasons, localized. The product's vocabulary and the SDK's API contract. Real mechanism: distilled on-device models for privacy/latency; GPU server ensembles for heavy authenticity checks; per-language calibration; every decision logged for the quarterly public report.

Ask KinShield ★ P0

Paste, forward, photograph or dictate anything → verdict + action in your language in seconds. Real mechanism: share-sheet + keyboard extension + widget entry points; on-device OCR → language ID → engine → action buttons (block/report/alert family/Incident).

SMS / RCS Shield P0

Scam texts filtered before the inbox. Real mechanism (the key OS detail): iOS ILMessageFilterExtension classifies messages from unknown senders only in a sandbox; Android takes the default-SMS or spam role. On-device multilingual classifier + private-relay lookup when confidence is low.

Link & QR Checker P0

Paste or scan; verdict in 2s. Real mechanism: domain age / WHOIS / certificate-transparency / homoglyph detection vs a Canadian brand whitelist; in-app camera scanner (built); MV3 browser extension.

VerifyCA ★ P1

Check investment & job offers against the CSA registration search, provincial regulators, and corporate registries. Real mechanism: registry scraper/API layer with freshness monitoring + entity resolution (the hard part) + newcomer-language explanations.

Multilingual Defense ★ P0–P1

Twelve languages where losses concentrate. Real mechanism: Whisper-class ASR + per-language scam corpora built with settlement-agency partners; a language ships only when paid native speakers validate it.

Zero-Day Anomaly Signals P1

Per-user baselines flag what's abnormal for you, catching unlabeled scams. Real mechanism: on-device isolation-forest baselines → anomaly score into the Trust Engine; weekly recalibration.

Calls & Voice

The KinShield Line ★ P1

An AI receptionist screens unknown callers and patches through / warns / blocks per family policy. Real mechanism (off-handset): conditional call-forwarding of unknown callers to a CPaaS cloud number (Twilio/Telnyx) → streaming ASR → Trust Engine → policy engine → warm transfer with whisper announcement. Handset-independent — works on every iPhone and Android.

Caller Intelligence P0

Label / silence / block flagged numbers before the ring. Real mechanism: iOS Call Directory Extension + Live Caller ID Lookup (privacy-preserving); Android caller-ID role; STIR/SHAKEN attestation shown as a badge.

Assist Mode ★ P0–P1

Live coaching on a call you answered. Real mechanism: user puts the call on speaker; the app analyses ambient audio via the microphone (permitted) entirely on-device — lawful under one-party consent; nothing leaves the phone.

Voice Lock P1

Family voiceprints verify in-app requests. Real mechanism: ECAPA-TDNN-class embeddings on-device (never raw audio); liveness challenge + anti-replay; Quebec CAI biometric declaration before launch there.

Honeypot Decoy Line ★ P2

Decoy AI personas waste scammer time & harvest scripts. Real mechanism: CPaaS decoy numbers + persona library on our own consent/recording footing; harvested intel feeds the corpus. Launches only behind counsel review (interception/CRTC); never uses a user's voice.

Family & Seniors

Family Circle ★ P0

The household is the unit of protection — up to 12 members, cross-platform alerts, policy engine, the senior never manages a password (family-share key recovery). Real mechanism: household graph + role/permission model consumed by every shield; audited approval log on the personal plane.

Senior Mode ★ · Scam Drill ★ · Briefing & Heat Map P0–P1

An alternate presentation layer every feature renders into; opt-in scam inoculation drills with a household scoreboard; a localized weekly threat briefing + live map (city-level, k-anonymity).

Money & Business

Check Before You Send ★ · Wire Guard ★ · Meeting Assist ★ P1–P2

The 10-second habit before money moves (recipient + three pressure questions + cooling-off); the SMB Arup-killer (out-of-band release ceremony: live selfie + spoken codeword + voice match — the deepfake can't pass the channel it doesn't control); a second opinion on high-stakes video calls (advisory flags + the procedural guardrail).

KinShield SDK ★ (the Bill C-15 product) · Insurance Benefit P1–P2

The same brain licensed to banks & credit unions: Trust Engine API + a C-15 control kit + threat-intel feed + FCAC reporting pipeline. A fraud-reimbursement benefit with a Canadian insurer, claims pre-evidenced by Incident Mode.

Privacy, Exposure & Recovery

Zero-Knowledge Vault ★ · Exposure Sweep · Passkey Coach P0–P1

E2EE personal data verified by a published audit; breach + data-broker sweeps with statutory Law-25 deletion demands and Shadow Contacts; a gamified household hardening program.

Incident Mode ★ · Crisis Vault ★ · Recovery Line ★ · Long-Con Radar ★ P0–P1

The panic button (guided first-hours flow, pre-filled CAFC/police, scripted bank calls, recovery-scam pre-arm); the sextortion first-four-hours flow (de-escalation + StopNCII); a trained multilingual human who joins bank calls for 3 months; staged detection of romance/pig-butchering over weeks.

Community Threat Network · Email Guard P0–P1

One consented report protects everyone in minutes (graph propagation, reputation decay, contributor karma); read-only OAuth inbox scanning with SPF/DKIM/DMARC forensics.

PART 10

The phased roadmap — step by step

Each stage has an entry condition, a budget, a task list, and an exit gate. Do not start a stage until the previous gate is passed — the gates stop you spending Stage-2 money on a Stage-0 idea.

Stage 0 — Validate (0–3 mo · $8–18K)

Steps: clear the name (CIPO + domains + collisions); incorporate federally + founder IP assignment; 100 discovery interviews (25 seniors, 25 adult children, 25 newcomers, 15 victims, 10 bank/CU risk staff); landing page + waitlist per language with $1–2K ads; a concierge "no-code" MVP (you manually check suspicious texts for 50 families for 4 weeks — builds the first labeled corpus by hand); one settlement-agency/seniors-org LOI; 2–3h with a privacy/fintech lawyer; apply for IRAP/SR&ED/Mitacs.

Challenge → resolution: a deck feels like progress while proving nothing → force the concierge test & interviews; if <15% call the problem serious or the concierge test doesn't get repeat sends, pivot the wedge for $5K, don't build.

Exit gate: ≥1,500 waitlist · 1 LOI · concierge users send suspicious content more than once · name cleared.

Stage 1 — MVP (3–9 mo · $90–350K)

Scope (zero app-store risk): SMS/RCS Shield, Link & QR, Ask KinShield, Trust Score v1 (4 languages), Family Circle + Senior Mode, Incident Mode, Community reporting. No Line, insurance, or SDK yet — those need the seed.

Team (hybrid): founder + 1 senior mobile contractor + 1 ML contractor + paid community validators.

Challenge → resolution: native message/call hooks & the first ML model are the two hard, narrow pieces → hire specialist contractors only for those; App Store/Play review for the SMS extension → prepare the sensitive-permission declaration + demo video early (month 3); Apple Live Caller ID entitlement queue → file in week one.

Exit gate: 10K installs · 500+ paying households (~$60K ARR) · D30 retention ≥25% · one documented, consented "money saved" story · partnership-sourced CAC <$20.

Stage 2 — Seed & scale (9–24 mo · $1.5–2.5M)

Ships, in order: call layers (Caller Intel, Assist, voicemail) + Exposure Sweep → the KinShield Line (limited→GA) + VerifyCA + Check Before You Send → Voice Lock (Quebec after CAI) + Crisis Vault + SDK pilot #1 with a credit union → Wire Guard + languages 9–12 + SDK pilot #2 + honeypot legal review + insurance-partner negotiations begin. Hire (in order): senior backend/telephony, ML, mobile, head of community partnerships, part-time compliance, 2 recovery staff.

Challenge → resolution: telephony cost & reliability → dual-CPaaS, minute budgets, paid-tier gating; SOC 2 is a sales prerequisite → start Type I in month 9; insurance underwriting is a 12-month pole → begin negotiations in P1.

Exit gate: $1M+ ARR · 2 SDK pilots signed · churn <3.5%/mo · SOC 2 Type I.

Stage 3 — Series A & category (yr 2–4 · $8–15M)

Goal: bank SDK revenue, the insurance benefit live, market leadership. Honeypot & Meeting Assist ship; SDK goes multi-tenant production; Big-5 / credit-union-central conversations on the back of SOC 2 Type II + live references.

Challenge → resolution: enterprise procurement & security review → the compliance artifact pack built as P1/P2 collateral; scaling the human Recovery Line → train-the-trainer + multilingual partner network.

Exit gate: $5–10M ARR · a Big-5 or credit-union-central deal · path to profitability.

PART 13

Claims discipline & ethics — the moat that costs nothing

The permanent rules — the integrity standard that is itself a competitive weapon:

  • Never publish an accuracy number without the benchmark, the date and the failure cases beside it.
  • Never imply protection on a channel the OS doesn't permit (the Line screens what's routed to it; Assist helps when invoked — and the marketing says exactly that).
  • Never attribute a breach or failure to a competitor without primary-source verification.
  • Never promise insurance outcomes — "designed to be fast", never "instant" or "no proof of loss".
  • Never use invented pilot data; never collect what the personal plane cannot protect; never ship a language whose detection quality isn't validated by paid native speakers.
In a market built on "96%" and "99.2%", the company that publishes its calibration curves — misses included — becomes the one journalists cite, regulators trust, banks shortlist, and families recommend. That is the genius this product runs on: not more claims than the giants, but claims that are true.
